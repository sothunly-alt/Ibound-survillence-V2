2. Scope of Service

Inbound Surveillance provides on-premise edge appliances and related software for internal garage labor accounting, bay occupancy auditing, and workflow automation. Services are sold as B2B pilots and deployments to facility operators (Data Controllers). Mechanics are not end-user account holders.

3. Zero-Cloud / Local Data Minimization

Local Data Minimization Notice

Inbound Surveillance operates strictly as a closed on-premise appliance. Raw RTSP video streams and biometric facial/skeletal vector embeddings are processed in local volatile memory (RAM) and discarded on-chip. No video footage, facial captures, or employee biometric markers are ever egressed, streamed, or stored on external cloud infrastructure. Only encrypted numerical telemetry logs (session duration, timestamps, and aggregate wrench-time hours) are transmitted to the designated operator Telegram webhook.
