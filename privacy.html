Privacy Policy & Data Architecture Disclosure

Last Updated: September 2026

Roles

Data Controller
Garage Owner / Facility Operator — determines purposes and means of workplace processing.
Data Processor
Inbound Surveillance — supplies and configures on-premises edge appliances that process data solely on the Controller’s instructions and hardware.

1. System Architecture & Zero-Cloud Guarantee

Inbound Surveillance is engineered around local edge-compute infrastructure.

  • Local Processing: All computer vision pipelines, body-pose estimation, and facial identification algorithms execute exclusively on on-premises local edge hardware deployed within the client’s facility.
  • No Video Streaming: Continuous CCTV video streams are processed ephemerally in volatile memory (RAM) and are never transmitted to external cloud servers, third-party databases, or hosted remote storage.
  • Bandwidth & Transit: Network egress is restricted exclusively to authenticated payload triggers (e.g., encrypted Telegram bot webhooks delivering metadata and shift verification snapshots).

2. Categories of Data Processed

  • Biometric & Pose Telemetry: Ephemeral spatial coordinates (full-body posture tracking) used to maintain active labor session continuity, alongside cryptographic vector embeddings generated from facial features for operator authentication. Raw facial templates are stored locally in hashed, non-reversible binary formats.
  • Labor Telemetry: Timestamped event intervals (Session Start, Session Pause, Session Terminate), active bay IDs, and dual-clock calculations (Billable Project Time vs. Attendance/Payroll Time).
  • Verification Proof: Momentary, downscaled image captures generated solely upon session state transitions to serve as timestamped proof-of-work verification.

3. Purpose of Processing

Data processed by the Inbound Surveillance hardware is utilized strictly for:

  • Automating billable labor tracking and bay occupancy metrics.
  • Generating shift scorecards and operational summaries for garage management.
  • Eliminating manual interaction with physical terminals in industrial environments.

Data is never harvested, resold, aggregated for third-party advertising, or used to train external global foundation models.

4. Third-Party Transmissions (Telegram Integration)

Shift summaries, timestamped visual verifications, and bay status alerts are dispatched via encrypted HTTPS webhooks directly to client-configured endpoints (such as the Telegram Bot API). Once dispatched, the storage, retention, and access governance of transmitted scorecards adhere to the client’s internal messaging security policies and Telegram’s platform terms.

5. Data Retention & Erasure

  • Telemetry Logs: Raw operational event timestamps are retained on local hardware storage for a rolling retention cycle (default: 30 to 90 days) configured by the facility owner.
  • Local Media: Ephemeral frame buffers are overwritten continuously. Hashed enrollment profiles remain stored on the local appliance until manually revoked or deleted by the facility administrator.
  • System Purge: Upon termination of service, all locally stored vector databases, enrollment profiles, and shift history can be completely purged via an on-site factory hardware reset.

6. Facility Operator Obligations (Workplace Notice)

The purchasing entity (Garage Owner / Operator) serves as the primary Data Controller. Inbound Surveillance acts as Data Processor. The Controller acknowledges the following legal commitments:

  • Employee Notice: The operator is solely responsible for providing conspicuous workplace notification (e.g., posted signage in bays) indicating that active optical computer vision and automated timekeeping systems are operational.
  • Jurisdictional Compliance: The operator must ensure deployment complies with all applicable local labor statutes, workplace surveillance regulations, and employee consent standards.
