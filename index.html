[ 04 ] Field Notes

> FIELD DISCOVERY // PHNOM PENH GROUND TRUTH

Bespoke builds. Paper clipboards. Blind trust.

Direct bay audits across Mean Chey (Aeon 3 corridor), Koh Pich private tuning bays, and import garages. In every bay, the operational failure was identical: high-value custom work tracked by memory, word-of-mouth, and paper.

THE MARGIN BLEED

Custom Projects vs. Fixed Estimates

In high-end tuning and custom fabrication, every vehicle presents unique, unforeseen complications. When diagnostic and fabrication time isn't logged automatically, owners absorb the hit: custom hours slip off the final invoice, or fixed monthly technician salaries subsidize unbilled shop idleness.

THE TRUST CEILING

Paper, Word of Mouth, and Memory

Across the bays surveyed, zero digital management software was in use. Bay tracking relies entirely on clipboards, verbal updates, and unverified trust. Owners spend high-value hours manually grading staff, policing phone usage, and guessing who worked on which vehicle.

THE GREASE BARRIER

Physical Work Rejects Touch Interfaces

Mechanics working with fluids, degreaser, and hand tools refuse to de-glove to tap a tablet or scan a QR code. Traditional shop software fails within 48 hours of install because dirty hands reject clean screens. If time and safety tracking aren't invisible from the ceiling, they do not happen.

[ 07 ] FAQ

Questions owners ask before a bay install.

Why isn’t Face ID enough?

Faces disappear under hoods, on lifts, and behind masks. We use face recognition just to identify the mechanic, but our AI tracks their full-body movement to keep counting the billable hours even when their face is completely hidden. It’s built for real garages, not office desks.

Does the video leave the shop?

Never. We guarantee zero cloud storage. The video is analyzed entirely on a small box inside your shop and instantly deleted. The only thing that ever leaves the building is the simple daily text summary sent straight to your Telegram.

I already have cameras. Do I need new ones?

Usually not. If you have standard security cameras, our $99/month software can plug right into them. If you have blind spots or need a better view, we can install a high-definition commercial camera for a flat $295 upfront.

How fast can we set this up?

Fast. Because our team is based right here in Phnom Penh, we come to your shop and set it up for free. We handle the wiring, adjust the camera angles, and connect your Telegram alerts in a single afternoon.

How is this different from a time-clock app?

Standard apps only track when a mechanic is inside the building. We track exactly when they are actually working on a car. By capturing real wrench-time, you can accurately bill for custom projects without guessing. It’s a tool to recover your lost money, not an HR app.
