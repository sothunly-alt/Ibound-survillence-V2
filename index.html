[ 08 ] FAQ

Questions owners ask before a bay install.

Why isn’t Face ID enough?

Faces disappear under hoods, on lifts, behind grinding shields, and behind masks. Face ID is the handshake. YOLOv8 full-body pose keeps the session alive when the face is gone.

Does video leave the shop?

No. Inference runs on-premise. We do not stream bay video to a cloud NVR. End-of-day scorecards push metadata and summaries to your Telegram via @Inbound_Surveillance_bot.

What do the dual clocks mean?

One clock tracks billable client wrench-time inside the bay polygon. The other tracks shop attendance / payroll presence. Mechanics wrench — the edge node splits both without taps.

How fast does a demo happen?

We respond within one business day on Telegram (Phnom Penh time), then map a single bay for a live walkthrough. Software is $39/bay/mo; turnkey hardware install is $250 + the monthly rate.

Are staff notified?

Yes — you’re responsible for workplace notice. Our operator acknowledgment and Privacy Policy cover internal labor accounting only, not covert surveillance.
