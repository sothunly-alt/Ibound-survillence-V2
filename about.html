Inbound Surveillance Edge Systems // Phnom Penh, Cambodia

Inbound Surveillance is built by a Phnom Penh-based engineering squad deploying autonomous edge infrastructure directly to the garage floor. We don’t build cloud dashboards for air-conditioned offices; we solve the chronic leakage of unbilled labor where mechanics work with 10W-40, transmission fluid, and bare metal.

By coupling opportunistic facial recognition with unbreakable YOLOv8 full-body skeletal tracking, our on-premise hardware captures billable wrench-time to the second—delivering zero-click daily dispatch scorecards directly to shop owners over Telegram without a single tablet on the floor.

What we ship

  • Face ID as handshake — pose keeps the session alive under hoods and lifts.
  • Dual clocks: billable wrench-time and payroll attendance.
  • On-premise inference — no cloud video stream.
  • Telegram scorecards via @Inbound_Surveillance_bot.
