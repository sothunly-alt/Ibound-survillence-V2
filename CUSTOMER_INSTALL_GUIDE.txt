====================================================================
        INBOUND SURVEILLANCE - CHAMPEI SPA
        WINDOWS INSTALLATION GUIDE (ONE-CLICK)
====================================================================

TARGET PLATFORM: Windows 10 / Windows 11 (64-bit)

--------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT YOU RECEIVED
--------------------------------------------------------------------
A single installer file:

    Inbound Surveillance_0.1.6_x64-setup.exe

You do NOT need to install Python or any other software first. The
installer sets everything up on its own.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 1: INSTALL
--------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Double-click "Inbound Surveillance_0.1.6_x64-setup.exe".
2. If Windows shows a permission prompt, click "Yes".
3. Click "Install" and wait for it to finish.
4. When it is done, the app is installed and a shortcut is created.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 2: OPEN THE APP
--------------------------------------------------------------------
- Open "Inbound Surveillance" from the Start Menu (or the desktop
  shortcut).
- The dashboard opens automatically. There is NO login or password.
- NOTE: the first launch may take a few seconds while the camera
  engine starts.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 3: CONNECT TELEGRAM ALERTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------
1. In the dashboard, go to: Settings -> Integrations.
2. Click "Connect Telegram".
3. Telegram opens - tap "Start". The chat is now linked and alerts
   will be delivered there.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 4: ADD YOUR CAMERAS
--------------------------------------------------------------------
1. In the dashboard, open the camera settings.
2. Paste your camera / DVR link, for example:
      rtsp://username:password@192.168.1.10:554/stream1
3. Click "Connect". Live video and detections will begin.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
EVERYDAY USE
--------------------------------------------------------------------
- Open "Inbound Surveillance" whenever the venue is operating.
- Keep it running so alerts are sent.
- To close the system, simply close the app window.
