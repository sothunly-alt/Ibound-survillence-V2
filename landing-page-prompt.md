# Landing Page Design Prompt — Agentic Vision Platform

## Role
You are a senior product designer/frontend engineer. Build a single-page marketing website using the visual style described below, populated with the business details I provide at the end.

## Visual Style Reference
- **Theme:** Dark mode, futuristic, AI/tech aesthetic
- **Palette:** Black/near-black background (#0A0A0A – #111111), neon green accent (#4ADE80 / #22C55E), white text for headlines, muted gray for body copy
- **Typography:** Rounded, geometric sans-serif for headlines (bold, large scale); clean sans-serif for body text
- **Motifs:** Glowing 3D glass/lens shapes, abstract flowing ribbon or smoke graphics, soft neon glow effects, thin rounded-corner cards with subtle borders
- **Buttons:** Pill/rounded-rectangle, one filled (white or green) as primary CTA, one outlined/ghost as secondary CTA
- **Layout rhythm:** Full-bleed hero → logo strip/trust bar → feature cards (3-up) → about/value prop split section → testimonials (3-up cards with star ratings and quote marks) → content/blog cards (3-up with images) → contact/lead-gen section with a form and an illustrative graphic → footer with contact details

## Sections to Build

1. **Navbar**
   - Logo (left), nav links (Home, About, Solutions, Pricing, Contact), primary CTA button (right)

2. **Hero**
   - Small pill badge above headline (icon + one-line hook)
   - Large two-line headline (line 2 in accent green or lighter weight)
   - 1–2 sentence subheadline explaining the value prop
   - Two CTA buttons: primary ("Book a Demo" / "Start Free") + secondary ("See How It Works")
   - Background: abstract glowing 3D shapes suggesting cameras/vision/AI

3. **Trust/Problem Strip**
   - Short line naming the market gap and who it's for

4. **Feature Cards (3-up)**
   - Each card: icon, title, 2-sentence description, "See More →" link
   - Map to core product pillars (e.g., Safety Wedge, Loss Prevention, Operational Insights)

5. **About / Value Prop (split section)**
   - Small "About Us" pill label
   - Two-line big statement headline
   - Paragraph describing the mission/approach
   - 4 small sub-feature blocks (icon/title/short line each) — e.g., Zero-CapEx, Edge AI, Instant Alerts, Scalable Margin

6. **Testimonials (3-up)**
   - Quote mark icon, name, title/company, star rating, 2–3 sentence quote
   - "View More →" link below

7. **Insights / Blog Cards (3-up)**
   - Image, title, 1-line teaser, arrow link icon
   - "View More →" link below

8. **Contact / Lead Capture**
   - "Contact Us" pill label
   - Headline ("Tell Us Your Goals" style)
   - Form fields: Name, Email, Phone, Message, Submit button
   - Decorative illustration beside the form (glowing brain/eye/camera motif tying to "vision")

9. **Footer**
   - Quick contact info (phone, email, location), secondary nav links, social icons

## Instructions
- Use the business details block below to fill in all copy — do not invent claims outside what's given.
- Keep headlines punchy and benefit-driven, not feature-driven.
- Reflect the zero-CapEx / no-hardware-replacement angle prominently in hero or about section, since it's the core differentiator.
- Reflect the "$20/camera/month" pricing and margin story only if a pricing section is requested — otherwise keep it out of hero copy.
- Output as a single responsive HTML/CSS (or React, if specified) file matching the visual style above.
